FAQ

Common questions

How fast do you detect an outage?

Probes run every 30 seconds on the Team plan and below 10 seconds on Enterprise. Median alert time across all customers is under 30 seconds from the first failed check.

Can I monitor internal, non-public endpoints?

Yes — install a lightweight local agent to expose internal services to Kabuti Grid without opening them publicly.

What happens if a region itself goes down?

Checks are cross-verified from at least two other regions before an alert fires, so a single region issue never triggers a false page.

Do you offer a free trial on paid plans?

Team plan includes a 14-day free trial, no card required. Enterprise trials are scoped with your team during onboarding.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, monthly plans can be cancelled anytime from your dashboard — no lock-in contracts on Starter or Team.
